Privacy

Cube Dash doesn't collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, music, haptics), your best score for each mode, and which vehicle and track you're using. They're stored on your device and never sent anywhere by the game.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your best runs are posted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center name. That's handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer doesn't receive it.

Purchases

Purchases go through the App Store. The game only asks the App Store which items you own, so it can unlock them.

What there isn't

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code, and no data shared with anyone.

Children

Because nothing is collected, Cube Dash is safe for players of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page and the App Store listing will say so first.
